🏞️ Land & Soil - Complete Assessment Guide

📋 Land Assessment Fundamentals

1. Topography & Slope Analysis

  • Flat land (0-2%): Easy to work, water pools, needs drainage
  • Gentle slope (2-10%): Ideal for water flow, runoff management
  • Moderate slope (10-20%): Needs swales/terraces, erosion potential
  • Steep slope (>20%): Erosion risk, requires special techniques

2. Aspect & Sun Exposure

  • North-facing: Cool, moist, good for shade plants
  • South-facing: Warmest, best for sun-loving plants (N. hemisphere)
  • East-facing: Morning sun, afternoon shade
  • West-facing: Afternoon heat, hardy plants

3. Water Flow & Drainage

Observe during heavy rain:

  • Mark natural water flow patterns
  • Identify low points for ponds
  • Note waterlogging areas
  • Look for natural streams
  • Test soil drainage (dig hole, fill with water, time drainage)

🔬 Comprehensive Soil Testing

Testing Components:

  • Macronutrients: N, P, K, Ca, Mg, S
  • Micronutrients: Fe, Mn, Cu, Zn, B, Mo
  • Organic Matter: 3-5% ideal
  • CEC (Cation Exchange Capacity): Nutrient-holding ability
  • Texture: Sand, silt, clay percentages
  • Salinity: Salt concentration

Testing Timeline:

  • New Land: Before any work begins
  • Ongoing: Every 2-3 years
  • Problem Areas: Anytime you notice issues
  • Best Timing: Fall (allows adjustment before spring)
  • Sampling: 8-10 spots mixed for average

🔬 Soil pH - Complete Guide

📊 Understanding pH Scale

0-7 = ACIDIC (more hydrogen ions)

7 = NEUTRAL

7-14 = ALKALINE/BASIC (more hydroxide ions)

Each point = 10x change in acidity/alkalinity!

🌾 pH by Crop Type

pH Range Type Best Crops Nutrient Availability
4.0-5.0 Very Acidic Blueberries, azaleas Iron & manganese available
5.5-6.0 Acidic Most vegetables Good nutrient availability
6.0-7.0 Slightly Acidic (IDEAL) Most crops Optimal nutrient release
7.0-8.0 Neutral to Alkaline Legumes, brassicas Iron & manganese locked up
8.0+ Very Alkaline Limited crops Most nutrients unavailable

🧪 Testing Methods

  • DIY pH Kit (~$10): Quick, moderate accuracy, instant results
  • Digital pH Meter (~$30-100): Better accuracy, needs calibration
  • Lab Testing (~$20-50): Most accurate + full nutrient analysis, 1-2 weeks

⚙️ Adjusting pH

To RAISE pH (Lower Acidity):

  • Add agricultural limestone (takes 3-6 months)
  • Wood ash (use sparingly, 5-10 tons/acre)
  • Ground oyster shells or coral
  • Apply 1-2 tons per acre per pH point

To LOWER pH (Increase Acidity):

  • Add sulfur (2-4 tons/acre per pH point)
  • Acidifying fertilizers (ammonium sulfate)
  • Peat moss (20-30% of top 6 inches)
  • Pine needles or oak leaves

💡 Pro Tips

  • Test soil in fall to adjust for spring planting
  • Recheck every 2 years once stable
  • Small pH adjustments take time - be patient
  • Add compost (buffers pH naturally)
  • Problem crops often indicate pH issues first

Crop Plant Harvest Days Notes

Crop Plant Harvest Days Notes

🍂 FALL (September-October)

Crop Plant Harvest Days Notes
Broccoli Mid-summer 55-70 days 55-70 Start indoors, transplant in July
Cabbage Mid-summer 60-90 days 60-90 Storage crop, stores well
Kale Mid-summer 50-65 days 50-65 Cold hardy, sweeter after frost
Radish Late summer 25-30 days 25-30 Quick crop, succession plant
Turnip Late summer 50-75 days 50-75 Root storage, cool season

❄️ WINTER (November-February)

Cool Season Crops (if unheated greenhouse):

  • Microgreens: Indoors, year-round, 10-14 days
  • Sprouts: Indoors, 5-7 days, no soil needed
  • Winter lettuce: Cold frame/greenhouse
  • Mâche: Very cold hardy, outdoor
  • Asian greens: Cold tolerant, outdoor

📍 Succession Planting Strategy

Plant same crop every 2-3 weeks for continuous harvest:

  • Lettuce: Every 2 weeks
  • Beans: Every 3 weeks
  • Corn: Every 2-3 weeks
  • Radish: Every week

🐟 Fisheries - Complete Aquaculture Guide

🐠 Popular Fish Species

Species Temperature Growth Rate Best For
Tilapia 75-85°F Fast Beginners, warm climate
Catfish 75-85°F Medium Bottom feeding, hardy
Carp 65-75°F Slow-Med Large, long-lived
Trout 55-65°F Medium Cold water, oxygenation needed

💧 Water Quality Parameters

Parameter Ideal Effect if Wrong
pH 6.5-8.0 Low=toxic, High=nutrient lock
Dissolved O₂ 5-8 ppm Low stress/death
Ammonia (NH₃) 0-0.02 ppm Toxic even low levels
Nitrite (NO₂) 0 ppm Deadly poison

🍽️ Stocking Density

  • Tilapia: 100-200 per 100 gallons
  • Catfish: 50-100 per 100 gallons
  • Carp: 30-50 per 100 gallons
  • Trout: 20-50 per 100 gallons (high oxygen)

📋 Maintenance Schedule

  • Daily: Check fish behavior, feed
  • Weekly: Monitor water level, remove debris
  • Monthly: Test pH, ammonia, dissolved O₂
  • Quarterly: 25% water change
  • Annually: Clean inlets/outlets, repair

🌾 Farming Types - 12 Approaches

1. Organic Farming

No synthetics, certification required, 3-year transition. Scale: Any | Cost: Moderate-High

2. Agroforestry

Trees + crops/livestock. Alley cropping, silvopasture. 5-10 year ROI

3. Market Gardening

Intensive vegetables, 0.5-5 acres, direct-to-consumer. High profit/acre

4. Grain Farming

Large-scale monoculture, 100+ acres. Commodity-price dependent

5. Livestock Farming

Grazing, intensive, or rotational. Regenerative potential

6. Apiculture

Beekeeping, urban-friendly. Multiple products: honey, wax, propolis

7. Specialty Crops

Vineyards, nuts, berries. Premium pricing, long establishment

8. Hydroponics

Soilless, controlled environment. 10-20x yield vs traditional

9. Aquaculture

Fish/shellfish farming. High protein production

10. Regenerative Ag

Cover crops, rotation, no-till. Carbon sequestration focus

11. Mushroom Farm

Controlled environment, uses waste. $10-20/lb, quick ROI

12. Medicinal Plants

Ginseng, echinacea. Premium pricing (10-100x commodity)
