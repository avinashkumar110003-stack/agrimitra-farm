🌍 About Our Farm

Our permaculture farm is designed as an integrated agricultural ecosystem. The aim is to produce food while conserving soil, water, energy and biodiversity.

🌱 Sustainable Farming

The farm uses resources carefully and aims to reduce unnecessary waste.

🌳 Biodiversity

Trees, crops, animals and beneficial organisms are connected within the farm system.

♻️ Resource Recycling

Organic materials can be converted into compost and returned to the soil.

🔄 Closed-Loop System

Different farm activities are connected so useful resources can circulate within the farm.

📐 Farm Plans

🌱 1-Acre Farm

A compact system can combine a food forest, vegetable garden, composting, water management, small livestock areas and a pond where feasible.

🌳 5-Acre Farm

A larger system can provide more space for food forests, orchards, livestock, water bodies, windbreaks, solar infrastructure and biodiversity.

📌 Important

The final layout should depend on land shape, soil, rainfall, water availability, slope and farm requirements.

🔄 Closed-Loop Farm System

🌧️ Rain falls on the farm
↓
💧 Water is captured and stored
↓
🌱 Plants use water and nutrients
↓
🌾 Crops produce food and biomass
↓
🐄 Animals produce manure
↓
♻️ Organic materials become compost
↓
🌱 Compost supports healthy soil
↓
🔄 New plants grow and the cycle continues
