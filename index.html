🌍 Land & Soil Chemistry Guide

🏞️ Land Assessment

Understanding your land's characteristics is crucial for successful permaculture design. Evaluate slope, aspect, water flow, existing vegetation, soil depth, and microclimates. Document seasonal weather patterns and existing infrastructure.

📊 Soil pH Levels

Soil pH affects nutrient availability and plant growth. Most crops prefer 6.0-7.0 pH. Test your soil regularly and adjust with lime (to raise) or sulfur (to lower). Understanding pH helps optimize your farm's productivity.

🔍 Soil Testing

Regular soil testing provides essential data about nutrient content, pH, organic matter, and microorganisms. This information guides your composting strategy and fertilization decisions for optimal farm health.

📈 Ideal pH Ranges for Common Farm Plants

Most Vegetables: 6.0-7.0
Blueberries: 4.5-5.5
Carrots: 6.0-6.8
Apples: 6.0-7.0
Potatoes: 5.5-6.8
Berries: 4.5-6.0
